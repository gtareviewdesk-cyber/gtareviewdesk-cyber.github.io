FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you post replies without my approval?

No. Every reply is drafted for your approval first. Nothing is published on your Google Business Profile until you approve it.

Are the replies written by AI?

We use AI-assisted writing tools to prepare first drafts. A person then reviews and edits each draft to match your voice and the specific review before it's sent to you for approval.

Do I need to share my Google password?

Never. If you'd like us to post approved replies, you can add our account as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. Managers can't delete your profile or manage its users, and you can remove our access at any time. If you'd rather post replies yourself, we'll simply send you the approved text.

Will you write reviews for my business or remove bad ones?

No. We only write replies to genuine reviews your customers have left. We don't write, buy, or solicit fake reviews, and we can't remove reviews from Google.

How do you handle negative reviews?

We flag them to you right away with a calm, professional suggested reply that acknowledges the concern and invites the customer to continue the conversation offline. You decide what gets posted.

What if I get more than 20 reviews in a month?

We'll let you know and agree on next steps with you before drafting replies beyond 20. You will never be charged extra automatically.

Can I cancel the monthly service?

Yes, anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you won't be billed again. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses across the Greater Toronto Area, such as trades and home services, clinics, salons, and auto repair shops. If you're unsure whether we're a fit, just ask.
